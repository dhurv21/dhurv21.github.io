Failure Wiki

Reading about the post office scandal which happened in the UK due to technical glitches caused by software called ‘Horizon’, developed by the Japanese company Fujitsu, led to one of the most devastating institutional failures.

This scandal made me realise how comfort or ignorance of something can lead to disastrous outcomes. Unfortunately, over a thousand sub-postmasters were wrongfully prosecuted and some even comitted the suicide. People placing their comfort in the assumption that technology is always accurate is vastly the reason why these events happened in the first place.

Also, it was disturbing to know how little power the subpostmaster held in this system. Even after it was known to the authorities that something was wrong with the system, no one inside the system questioned it, whereas they continued using the software and blamed the subpostmasters.

Do tech companies only hold responsibilities of building the product? Don’t they have an ethical and moral duty of accounting for what happens when something goes wrong with their software?

Personally, this has made me more cautious about placing blind trust in technology or authority. Trust should be earned and constantly questioned because once you are in trouble, it would be really difficult to speak against it.
