Reflection on Inclusive terminology Presentation

Out of all presentations, the presentation on inclusive terminology caught my eye. I never had thought about it that way because for instance when you hear blacklist, the only thing I can make out of the word is litreally blacklist, or someone or something getting blacklisted. However, I still feel it’s kind dumb because I feel this example particularly is inculcating the belief of black as in blacklist being racist which never once striked my mind. I have only ever associated it with someone or smothing being banned or blocked. Well, as the presentation continued, I got to know that inclusive terminolgy is not just whether a person personally finds them offfensive; it’s about how words/language might shape society and perception beyond meaning or emotion. Even though most people do not associate the term blacklist with negative ideas, the argument is that it is repeated association of black with negative meaning and white with postive meaning can reinforce the certain bias in the society. I appreciated the critical thinking in the presentaion about somthing I never really thought about and how language evovles overtime with society. It kinds of matches with the motto of our university as well:)
